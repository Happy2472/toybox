ToyBox Photo Maker

Step 1: Pick the product photo

Best results: one item per photo, with space around it. Already cut out in another app? Choose that PNG and it is used as is.

Draw a box around the item

Drag your finger from one corner to the opposite corner. Leave a little table showing around the item, and keep other items outside the box.

Step 2: Adjust it

Drag the product with your finger to move it.

If part of the product disappears, slide left. If background is left behind, slide right.

Your picture is ready

Press and hold the picture, then tap Download image.
